# CNG-Protect 🛡️
### Intelligent IoT Safety Monitoring for Compressed Natural Gas Vehicles

## 📖 Project Overview
**CNG-Protect** is a specialized IoT solution designed to accelerate the adoption of Compressed Natural Gas (CNG) by eliminating its primary barrier: the fear of explosion.

As fuel prices for traditional petrol soar, economies are rapidly pivoting to CNG, which offers a massive reduction in operational costs—often saving drivers between **40% to 60%** compared to standard gasoline. However, this economic lifeline comes with a psychological and physical cost: the volatile risk of high-pressure cylinder failure. Leaks in the manifold or overheating storage tanks can lead to catastrophic explosions.

**CNG-Protect** bridges this gap by providing an active, real-time safety shield. By combining affordable embedded systems with cloud technology, we offer a scalable, life-saving innovation that builds the necessary trust for the clean energy revolution.

## 🚀 Key Features
* **Active Gas Monitoring:** Uses a precision **MQ-2 Gas Sensor** to detect micro-leaks of combustible gas (methane) long before they reach explosive concentrations.
* **Thermal Regulation:** A **DHT11 sensor** continuously monitors the thermal environment around the cylinder to detect dangerous temperature spikes.
* **Mobile App Dashboard:** Unlike passive valves, this system syncs data to a custom **mobile application**, serving as a "Vehicle Health Dashboard" for the driver.
* **Real-Time Cloud Logging:** Powered by **Google Firebase**, the system logs sensor data for historical analysis and predictive maintenance.

## 🛠️ Technical Stack
* **Hardware:** ESP32 Microcontroller, MQ-2 Gas Sensor, DHT11 Temperature/Humidity Sensor.
* **Connectivity:** Wi-Fi / Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE).
* **Backend:** Google Firebase (Realtime Database).
* **Interface:** Custom Mobile Application.
